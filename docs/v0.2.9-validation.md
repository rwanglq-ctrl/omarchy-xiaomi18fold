  edits. Match exact released/interim defaults, record the installed baseline,

- Rime upgrades must recognize the released v0.2.8 preset/fallback, not only the
  interim version. Pin exact hashes and test frozen historical fixtures. Deploy

- Rime may reuse the distribution's precompiled Luna table; assert its presence

  `user.yaml` content from the idempotence snapshot because its build timestamp
  changes; all other Rime config/build hashes and all path/mode checks remain.
